Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

Welcome to our platform. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, protect, and process your personal information when you use our website or services. By creating an account or using our platform, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

We may collect the following information:

  • Full Name, Email Address, and Mobile Number

  • Profile Photo (if uploaded)

3. Identity Verification Information

If KYC verification is required, we may collect documents such as: Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport, Driving License, Voter ID, Selfie Verification, and Address Proof. These documents are collected only for identity verification and fraud prevention.

4. Financial Information

Depending on the services you use, we may collect:

  • UPI ID and Bank Account Details

5. Device Information

For security and service improvement, we may automatically collect your Device Name, Device Model, Operating System, Browser Name, Screen Resolution, and App Version.

6. Network Information

We may collect IP Address, Login Time, Logout Time, Device ID, Country, State, City (Approximate), and Internet Service Provider. This information helps improve account security and detect suspicious activity.

Our website may use cookies to: Keep users logged in, Improve website performance, Remember preferences, Enhance security, and Analyze website traffic. Users can manage cookies through their browser settings.

8. How We Use Your Information

Your information may be used to: Create and manage your account, Verify your identity, Process deposits and withdrawals, Prevent fraud and abuse, Improve customer support, Send account-related notifications, and Enhance website performance and security.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect user information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. While we work to safeguard your data, no internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If changes are made, the revised version will be published on this website. Continued use of the platform after updates means you accept the revised Privacy Policy.

11. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share limited information only when necessary, such as: With payment providers to process transactions, With identity verification providers (if applicable), With service providers that help operate our platform, or When required by applicable law or a valid legal process.

Our platform may use third-party services including Payment gateways, Email services, SMS providers, Cloud hosting, and Analytics tools. These providers may process certain information according to their own privacy policies.

When you make a deposit or withdrawal, we may store: Transaction ID, UPI ID, Bank Account Details, Payment Method, Deposit Amount, Withdrawal Amount, Payment Date & Time, and Payment Status. This information is used for verification, record keeping, and fraud prevention.

We retain user information only for as long as reasonably necessary to: Maintain user accounts, Process transactions, Meet legal or regulatory obligations, Resolve disputes, Prevent fraud, and Improve platform security. Depending on applicable law, you may have rights to: Access your personal information, Correct inaccurate information, Request deletion of eligible data, Update your account details, and Contact support regarding your privacy concerns.
